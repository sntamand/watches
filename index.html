How a repair runs

From your door to the bench and back.

01

Intake & inspection

Bring your watch in or mail it with our prepaid label. We log its condition and photograph it before anything is opened.

02

Diagnosis & quote

Our watchmaker identifies the issue and sends a written quote. Nothing is repaired until you approve it.

03

Approval

Approve by email or text. You can also ask questions or request photos of the movement at this stage.

04

Repair at the bench

Your watch is worked on by one watchmaker start to finish, not passed between stations.

05

Quality check & timing test

Every repair is timed across positions for several days before it's signed off.

06

Ready for pickup or return

We call or email when it's ready, and ship insured if you're not local.

About the workshop

Small enough to know your watch by name.

Smatpie Watches started as a single bench and a stubborn belief: a watch is a machine, and machines can be understood, opened, and made right again. We still work that way — one watchmaker per repair, from intake to timing test.

We sell watches for the same reason we fix them: because a good mechanical piece, looked after properly, will outlast whoever buys it. Everything in our shop is chosen with that in mind, and everything we repair is treated like it's meant to be worn for another fifty years.
